# Stable Desk research priorities

As of September 30, 2026. Analyst proposals based on a public-source baseline; no commercial terms, outreach or employer-private material. The rank is a qualitative research sequence, not a forecast or a commercial score.

## P01 — Deepen the existing QR corridor

**Recommendation:** investigate one incremental wallet or market corridor, starting from the existing Singapore merchant route. Grab's [November 18, 2025 MOU](https://www.grab.com/sg/press/others/grab-and-straitsx-sign-mou-to-explore-the-development-of-web3-wallets-and-stablecoin-based-settlement-layer-across-asia/) is explicitly exploratory. StraitsX's [Alipay+ / GrabPay case study](https://www.straitsx.com/case-study/alipay-grab) describes a Singapore integration live since October 2024. These are existing relationships; regional rollout is not established by the MOU.

The case study's growth figures are provider claims with unspecified measurement windows and base counts. They do not establish total XSGD payment adoption. KBank and Q Wallet are already in the [StraitsX cross-border directory](https://www.straitsx.com/partners/cross-border-payments); their live corridor scope requires confirmation. [Alipay+'s official identity page](https://www.alipayplus.com/about-us/) distinguishes the Ant International gateway from a consumer wallet.

**Next:** map one corridor's public launch status, repeat merchant demand, FX and redemption path, and exception costs. Define an incremental payment metric with period and denominator.

**Disproof:** no reachable compliant production corridor; weak repeat demand; or existing fiat rails deliver better all-in economics. Evidence confidence: moderate. Lineage: S01–S03 → E01–E04, E17 → P01 → D01; S17 → E20 supports organization identity.

## P02 — Test Nium's need for an SGD leg

**Recommendation:** prepare a product-gap memo on whether SGD treasury holders would benefit from an XSGD conversion and payout connector. Nium's [August 19, 2026 funding announcement](https://www.nium.com/blog/usdc-funding-global-payouts) describes USDC → USD → local-currency payouts. Its [Visa settlement pilot](https://www.nium.com/newsroom/nium-joins-visa-stablecoin-settlement-cross-border-payments) includes USDC; this is not evidence of XSGD acceptance.

[Open Standard's June 30 announcement](https://joinopenstandard.com/blog/introducing-open-usd) lists both Nium and StraitsX. Shared participation offers ecosystem context and competing token economics to investigate. **Bilateral relationship, signed terms, XSGD policy and buyer demand remain unknown.** Do not interpret the reviewed source set's silence as proof that no relationship exists.

**Next:** establish observable SGD treasury demand, supported token policy, SGD corridor eligibility and total conversion/compliance cost versus fiat funding.

**Disproof:** demand is mainly USD; token policy is closed; or an additional conversion layer erases the benefit. Evidence confidence: exploratory. Lineage: S12–S14 → E13–E15, E18 → P02 → D02.

## P03 — Measure liquidity before adding rails

**Recommendation:** benchmark conversion and redemption on existing routes before proposing another integration or liquidity incentive. [Coinbase's XSGD page](https://www.coinbase.com/xsgd) advertises XSGD/USDC and Base transfers. StraitsX [announced its Solana Foundation collaboration](https://www.straitsx.com/blog-post/straitsx-to-launch-both-xsgd-and-xusd-on-solanas-public-blockchain) in December 2025, then [announced XSGD and XUSD live on March 31, 2026](https://www.straitsx.com/blog-post/xsgd-and-xusd-live-on-solana). The older [transfer guide](https://www.straitsx.com/blog-post/how-to-get-xsgd) still labels Solana “coming soon”: later launch evidence takes precedence, but the precise supported account flow needs reconciliation.

The [Uniswap protocol directory listing](https://www.straitsx.com/partners/defi) does not establish a bilateral Uniswap Labs agreement. [Swap API documentation](https://docs.straitsx.com/docs/swap-api) supports pair and quote-expiry mechanics, not executable depth or access entitlement.

**Next:** collect read-only timestamped quotes at S$1k, S$10k and S$100k equivalents. Include spread, fees, slippage, authentic-token checks, redemption constraints and LP concentration. These sizes are proposed measurement points, not observed liquidity.

**Disproof:** current routes already meet target execution and exit needs; no incremental payment demand; or improvements require uneconomic subsidies. Evidence confidence: moderate. Lineage: S06–S11, S15 → E07–E12, E19 → P03 → D03.

Card programs remain contextual watchlist records: the [RedotPay case study](https://www.straitsx.com/case-study/from-launch-to-scale-redotpays-wallet-ready-card-program) describes XUSD settlement. It does not establish XSGD card spend. Nothing in this baseline warrants treating token supply growth, listings or aggregate transfers as payment adoption.
