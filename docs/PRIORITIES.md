# Stable Desk example research brief

As of September 30, 2026. **Generic Stablecoin (STABLE) is fictional.** These three priorities are synthetic demonstrations of a research process, not evidence-backed commercial opportunities for an operating issuer. The qualitative order demonstrates reducing uncertainty before adding infrastructure. Every named real product retains its actual asset, source and date.

Source access establishes what the original public pages say, not independent verification of performance, reserves, executable liquidity or current account eligibility. Undated pages stay undated; historical releases are not detected changes. Token supply, total onchain transfers, card tender and merchant settlement are different measures. No adoption inference or commercial score is made from them. A production recommendation would need a real issuer profile, original-source relationship evidence, a specific user problem and current operating constraints.

V2 makes the assumption layer explicit: A-P-G01-1 links synthetic E-G11, while A-P-G01-2 links its original public market context. P-G02 and P-G03 follow the same synthetic-hypothesis/public-context split. Claim revisions show exactly which of these assumptions and decisions need review; source checks alone do not endorse an example. Local edits keep their own actual reviewer, date, rationale and before/after lineage.
